# Bangalore: five views of a lake corridor

Follow Bangalore's connected lake corridor from June to October 2026. Five observations let us inspect the progression between the endpoints: lake margins, neighboring vegetation and the strong returns of the surrounding city. Each view comes from an individually extracted NISAR radar file, with identical colours and map extent.

The five dates reveal changes in measured radar response across the season. Use the first-date comparison to keep lake margins and nearby land in view as you step through the sequence. Moisture, vegetation, roughness and urban surfaces can all affect the signal; a changing colour alone does not confirm inundation or a change in water quality. This sequence is a reproducible starting point for checking lake dynamics against rainfall, optical images and local observations.
