# Jeddah: five visits to a dry city's drainage landscape

Follow five NISAR visits from June to October 2026 across eastern Jeddah. Step through dry-season and autumn observations to compare broad drainage features, open land and built surfaces. All five images use one radar channel, one geographic grid and one fixed colour scale, so a new colour stretch cannot imitate a change.

This sequence builds a multi-date baseline for investigating later runoff events. A change in radar response can identify a place to examine, but smooth dry ground, terrain shadows and urban geometry can resemble or obscure water. No flood event has been established for these dates. Confirmed storm timing, rainfall observations and independent imagery are needed to turn this baseline into an event story; short-lived runoff may disappear between satellite visits.
