# Record Water on the Manning

Owner: sk.

From 20–25 May 2025 a slow-moving trough produced a documented 1-in-500-year flood on the NSW Mid North Coast. The Manning River at Taree peaked near 6.4 m on 21 May, above the 1929 record, with major flooding at Wingham and widespread rescues. These four Sentinel-1 radar visits follow a focused Manning corridor around Taree and Wingham from before the crest through late June.

## Interpretation

The 21 May acquisition falls on the documented crest day at Taree; it is still a radar brightness map, not a measured water depth or validated inundation polygon. Later frames show follow-up landscape response and must not be read as a full recovery certificate. Standing water, wet soil, crops and viewing geometry all affect brightness. No flood-area accuracy or damage estimate is claimed.
