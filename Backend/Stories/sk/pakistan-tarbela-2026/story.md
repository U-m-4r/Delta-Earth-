## Interpretation

Compare the shoreline and neighboring terrain through the actual acquisition dates. Radar response does not measure stored water volume or establish a flood. Wind, vegetation, roughness and geometry can influence brightness.

NISAR, consistent polarization and orbit per story. Source calibrated power converted using 10*log10 where necessary; existing dB sources are not converted again. Bilinear alignment to the first grid, invalid/nonpositive power masked, fixed viridis −30 to +5 dB display. Statistics are radar response, not classified flood area.
