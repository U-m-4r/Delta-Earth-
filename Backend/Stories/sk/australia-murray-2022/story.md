# When the Murray Spread Beyond Its Banks

Owner: sk.

During the 2022 eastern Australian floods, water moved through the Murray–Darling Basin over weeks rather than a single moment. NASA reported major flooding around Echuca in late October, and the basin authority subsequently reviewed the 2022–23 river flood. These four radar visits follow a defined area around Echuca and Moama across the lead-up and the weeks following the reported crest.

## Interpretation

The dates bracket the documented flood period, but there is no compatible 19 October frame in this selected orbit sequence: the record jumps from 7 to 31 October. The 31 October image follows the reported local crest and must not be labelled the peak itself. Radar response can change with standing water, wet soil, crops, flooded woodland and viewing geometry. This story does not calculate flood depth, damage or validated inundation area.
