# When the White River Broke Its Century Mark

Owner: sk.

From 11–17 August 2026 repeated thunderstorms dropped more than a foot of rain across parts of central Indiana. The White River set new records at Anderson and Noblesville, exceeding crests not seen since 1913, and Indianapolis declared a local disaster emergency. These four NISAR observations follow a focused corridor near Noblesville from late July through early September on one ascending track.

## Interpretation

The 16 August acquisition falls during the documented crest window; it measures radar response on a common grid and does not itself prove flood depth or mapped inundation extent. Later acquisitions provide follow-up views only. Vegetation, wet soil, urban surfaces and geometry also change brightness. This story does not estimate flooded area, damage or evacuation needs.

NISAR, consistent polarization and orbit per story. Source calibrated power converted using 10*log10 where necessary; existing dB sources are not converted again.
