## Interpretation

This sequence shows terrain-corrected radar response across the event period. Moisture, vegetation and surface roughness also affect brightness. This is not a validated water classification or a national flood-area estimate. The processed area is the explicitly mapped local subregion.

Sentinel-1 RTC, consistent polarization and orbit per story. Source calibrated power converted using 10*log10 where necessary; existing dB sources are not converted again. Bilinear alignment to the first grid, invalid/nonpositive power masked, fixed viridis −30 to +5 dB display. Statistics are radar response, not classified flood area.
